# PhD Research Alignment

This repository adds **runtime enforcement** to the broader trustworthy-CPS program. The cross-domain benchmark studies diagnosis; the federated repo studies distributed learning; `automatic-formal-verification-cps` studies offline proof-oriented reachability; this repository studies online action filtering. A central thesis direction is to use digital-twin uncertainty and offline reachability certificates to parameterize adaptive runtime shields without sacrificing sound safety semantics.
