# Research Roadmap

## v0.2
QP-based filters for supported linear constraints, explicit nonconvex obstacle shields, multi-seed confidence intervals, computation-time benchmarks.

## v0.3
Hybrid-mode reachability, validated nonlinear propagation, digital-twin uncertainty calibration, attack/fault-aware intervention.

## v0.4
Learning-enabled nominal controllers wrapped by formal runtime assurance, offline certificate reuse, cross-domain generalization studies.

## v1.0 research target
A reproducible evaluation of when offline reachability + online runtime assurance yields stronger safety/service tradeoffs than detection-only or fallback-only resilience.
